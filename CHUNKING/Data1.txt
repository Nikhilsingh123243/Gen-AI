Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning are transforming the way computers understand and process human language. 
Natural Language Processing (NLP) enables machines to analyze text through techniques such as tokenization,
 part-of-speech tagging, chunking, parsing, and named entity recognition.
A typical NLP pipeline begins by dividing a document into sentences and words, removing stopwords,
and applying stemming or lemmatization. The processed text is then analyzed to identify noun phrases,
verb phrases, and other meaningful chunks. These chunks help extract information, classify documents,
answer questions, summarize content, translate languages, and improve chatbot responses.
 Modern NLP models use embeddings, transformers, attention mechanisms, and large datasets to understand context,
semantics, syntax, and relationships between words, resulting in more accurate and intelligent language applications.
